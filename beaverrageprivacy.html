Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Beaver Rage ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or process any personal information from users of this app. However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect certain information as described below.

Third-Party Services

Beaver Rage uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information to provide personalized ads and analytics. The types of information that may be collected include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage data
  • Ad interaction data

AdMob's data collection and use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which can be found at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and enable "Limit Ad Tracking"

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features (leaderboards, achievements), Apple's Game Center will collect and store gameplay data according to Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Data Storage

We store only the following information locally on your device:

  • High scores
  • Game settings (sound preferences)
  • In-app purchase status

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

In-App Purchases

If you make in-app purchases (such as removing ads), these transactions are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple according to their privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app uses AdMob with settings appropriate for general audiences.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is minimal security risk. All data stored locally is managed by iOS's secure storage mechanisms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and indicated by updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store support channel.

Your Consent

By using Beaver Rage, you consent to this privacy policy.
